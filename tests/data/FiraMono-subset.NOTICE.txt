FiraMono-subset.ttf is the Fira Mono subset Bevy ships as its default font (bevy_text). Fira Mono is copyright The Mozilla Foundation and Telefonica S.A., licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). It is used only by this crate's tests, so the rasterizer can be exercised without a system font.
